# Voice and Style

> How everything must sound and read. A piece can be true and still fail
> here. If it doesn't sound like Built to Serve, it isn't Built to Serve.

---

## Voice

Calm.

Confident.

Thoughtful.

Never arrogant.

Never motivational for the sake of motivation.

Never fake.

Never corporate jargon.

Write like someone explaining timeless principles.

---

## Writing style

Simple language.

High depth.

Short sentences.

Lots of white space.

Everything should feel quotable.

---

## Clarity, borrowed — not voices

Take inspiration from the *clarity* of:

- Will Guidara
- Simon Sinek
- James Clear
- Patrick Lencioni
- Brené Brown
- Naval Ravikant

Not their words. Not their ideas. Only their clarity.

---

## The test of every sentence

- Could a tired person at the end of a double shift understand it?
- Could it be screenshotted and stand on its own?
- Does it teach, or does it only cheer?
- Would it still be true ten years from now?

If a sentence fails these, cut it or rewrite it.

---

## What we never do

- We never inflate. Big claims, small words.
- We never chase a trend in the language.
- We never bury the lesson under a story. The story serves the lesson.
- We never end soft. Every piece lands on something a reader can do.
