# The Philosophy

> The worldview, stated plainly. Everything we publish must trace back to a
> line on this page.

---

## Who I am

My name is Guillermo Padilla.

I am a hospitality leader.

I build culture. I develop people. I create systems.

I believe businesses become extraordinary when ordinary people are given
extraordinary leadership.

Hospitality is not an industry. Hospitality is a way of living.

Leadership is not authority. Leadership is service.

Culture is not what is written on a wall. Culture is what people repeatedly do.

Discipline beats motivation.

Professionalism is a decision.

Mentality is a choice.

Service is the highest form of leadership.

Everything begins with serving others.

---

## Core purpose

We don't build restaurants.

People build culture.

Culture builds extraordinary businesses.

Develop leaders who create better workplaces by serving people first.

To become one of the world's most respected voices in hospitality

## The Four Pillars

Every piece of content stands on one of these four. If it stands on none,
it is not Built to Serve.

### 1. Hospitality

- Serve before expecting.
- Create memorable experiences.
- Care genuinely.
- Lead with empathy.
- Make someone's day better.
- Serve teammates before serving guests.
- Hospitality starts inside the team.

### 2. Mentality

- Attitude is always a choice.
- Own mistakes.
- Stay coachable.
- Solve problems.
- Never complain without proposing solutions.

### 3. Professionalism

- Integrity.
- Preparation.
- Excellence.
- Respect.
- Attention to detail.
- Represent something bigger than yourself.

### 4. Discipline

- Consistency.
- Standards.
- Habits.
- Execution.
- Do the work even when motivation disappears.

---

## Non-negotiables

Always early.

Always prepared.

Always respectful.

Always learning.

Always accountable.

Always improving.

Always serving.
