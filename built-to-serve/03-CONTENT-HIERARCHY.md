# Content Hierarchy

> Everything belongs somewhere. Nothing is allowed to exist alone.
>
> Every article belongs to a chapter.
> Every chapter belongs to a pillar.
> Every pillar belongs to Built to Serve.

The five parts below map directly to the folders in [`library/`](library/).
When a piece is finished, it is filed into the part it belongs to. A piece
without a home here does not yet have a reason to exist.

## Filing rule

Before publishing, name the part, the chapter, and the pillar. Write them at
the top of the piece (the template enforces this). If you cannot name all
three without strain, the piece is not ready — its idea is still unclear.
